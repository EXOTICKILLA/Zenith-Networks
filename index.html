Server Rules

Keep roleplay fun, fair and enjoyable for everyone.

01. Respect everyoneNo harassment, discrimination or unnecessary toxicity.
02. Stay in characterKeep situations roleplay-focused whenever you are in the city.
03. No RDM / VDMDo not randomly kill or intentionally run over players without valid RP.
04. No cheatingExploits, cheats and unfair third-party advantages are not allowed.
05. Value your lifeReact realistically when your character is placed in danger.
06. Follow staff directionsStaff decisions should be respected. Use support tickets for disputes.
07. No metagamingDo not use information your character would not reasonably know.
08. No powergamingDo not force actions or outcomes onto another player's character.
